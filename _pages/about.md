I am an Applied AI Engineer at **TCS Research**, Pune, with 3.5+ years of experience building
GenAI-driven solutions, agentic workflows, and scientific ML systems for complex industrial problems.

My work sits at a unique intersection: I combine **physics-based modeling** with **modern AI** — designing
systems where the laws of nature and learned representations work together. Specifically, I build
LLM-powered agentic workflows, RAG pipelines, and multi-agent systems to automate decision intelligence
in industrial settings, with a strong focus on **battery modeling and digital twins**.

On the scientific ML side, I develop Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), DeepONet, and FNO-based
frameworks for multi-physics simulations, achieving 20–30% improvements in prediction accuracy over
purely data-driven baselines. These surrogate models enable real-time deployment in Battery Management
Systems (BMS) and digital twin applications.

I hold a **B.Tech + M.Tech (Dual Degree)** in Mechanical Engineering with a specialization in
Product Design from **IIITDM Kancheepuram** (CGPA: 8.55), and have previously interned at
**Rocket ML** (Oregon, USA) on large-scale CFD simulations, and worked as a Project Assistant at
**IIT Ropar** on industrial flow optimization.

Beyond research, I am passionate about translating cutting-edge AI into deployable industrial solutions,
contributing to **journal publications**, **patent filings**, and **invited conference talks**.
I have filed patents on composite electrode design and urban farming systems, and published in
*ACS Applied Energy Materials* on sodium-ion battery modeling.

**Research Interests:** GenAI · Agentic AI · RAG Pipelines · LLM Fine-tuning · Physics-Informed Neural Networks · Digital Twins · Battery Modeling · Electrochemical Simulation · Scientific Computing

## Overview

High-fidelity **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)** simulations for industrial applications,
spanning petroleum flow, tundish design, and parallel HPC execution.

## Projects

### Rocket ML — Scientific Computing Intern (2021)
- Executed large-scale **multiphase CFD simulations** using OpenFOAM on HPC clusters,
  modeling petroleum applications under real industrial operating conditions.
- Developed **Python automation pipelines** for simulation setup, mesh generation, solver
  configuration, and post-processing on Linux/HPC environments.
- Applied **parallel computing (OpenMPI)** to achieve significant speedups on multi-core systems.

### IIT Ropar — Industrial Flow Optimization (2021–2022)
- Performed **multiphase turbulence modeling** (VOF + k-ε) to study flow behavior in
  steelmaking tundish systems.
- Designed and evaluated **modified outlet geometries** to reduce vortex formation and
  prevent slag entrainment into liquid steel.
- Delivered actionable engineering recommendations that improved metal flow stability
  and casting quality.

## Technologies

`OpenFOAM` · `Python` · `OpenMPI` · `Linux (HPC)` · `ANSYS` · `Paraview` · `MATLAB`

## Impact

Demonstrated the ability to bridge computational simulation with practical engineering problems,
forming the foundation for my later work in scientific ML and physics-informed AI models.
